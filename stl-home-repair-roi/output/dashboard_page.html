Sources, methods and limits
  • USAspending.gov API: prime awards and sub-awards for CDBG (14.218), CDBG-DR (14.228), HOME (14.239), Lead Hazard Control (14.900, 14.905), Healthy Homes (14.906, 14.913), Older Adult Home Modification (14.921), Weatherization (81.042), LIHEAP (93.568) and USDA 504 (10.417, 10.433), for recipients or places of performance in St. Louis City (29510) and County (29189).
  • City of St. Louis CAPERs 2018–2025 (Community Development Administration): IDIS PR26 voucher detail and Healthy Home Repair narratives. In 2025, CDBG paid for 415 repairs across 57 homeowners, about $22K per home and 7 repairs each.
  • HUD CPD open data (CDBG and HOME activity layers) was checked. It leaves out single-family rehab, so it is not used for costs.
  • American Housing Survey 2019, 2021, 2023 national files: 82,656 contractor-done jobs. No St. Louis metro sample exists in these years, so the Midwest older-home subset stands in.
  • UMSL Community Innovation and Action Center, Home Repair Study (May 2023): repair needs and costs for older St. Louis homeowners.
  • ROI evidence: Gould (2009), Environmental Health Perspectives 117(7), lead hazard control returns $17–$221 per $1. DOE/ORNL Weatherization Assistance Program national evaluations: energy savings-to-investment ratio 1.4, benefit-cost 4.1 with health and safety. CAPABLE (Johns Hopkins; Bishai et al. 2018, JAGS): about $867 per month lower Medicaid spending against a $2,825–$5,000 program cost.
  • Limits: sub-awards are often annual program allocations, not single jobs. The keyword classification is approximate. The value model is correlational. Roof, furnace and electrical work have no published ROI study; they rank on value per job and local need.
