Rocket Crew

Seven team missions on one phone. About ten minutes. Everyone has a job, and the rocket only gets home if the whole crew helps.

  1. Lay the phone flat on a table or the floor.
  2. Sit two crew at each end. Each of you owns one corner of the screen.
  3. Turn the sound up and switch on rotation lock.

Age sets how hard each player's part is, so a 4-year-old and a 12-year-old can both shine.

Grown-ups: the II button on the middle stripe pauses, skips a mission or turns the voice off. Tap the middle stripe during a mission briefing to hear it again.
